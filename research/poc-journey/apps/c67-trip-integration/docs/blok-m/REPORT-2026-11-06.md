## 2. Objective and evaluation questions

The POC addresses three questions.

1. Does the recommendation list respond to the traveller's preferences?
2. Do the proposed places have sourced attributes that support those preferences?
3. Can the selected places form a feasible itinerary with consistent visit durations, published opening rules, travel estimates, forecast intervals and a return deadline?

A recommendation is considered generic when it is insensitive to the profile. Popularity was recorded as contextual discussion only. No popularity score was derived from editorial inclusion, candidate IDs or MapKit result order.

## 4. Data preparation and methodology

### 4.1 Sourced venue catalog

The dated catalog contains **13 candidate identities**, including multiple areas within shared complexes such as Taman Literasi/library and M Bloc/cafe. This inventory falls short of establishing the planned 12–18 independent, sufficiently documented venues.

Five candidates lack usable, separately sourced opening hours and remain outside schedulable proposals. Source records distinguish operator publications, government publications and independent directories. A recent retrieval date records what a publication says. Older publication dates, holiday exceptions, queues, capacity and temporary access still limit operational certainty.

The Mac MapKit probe found a center POI and twelve candidates. Toko Buku Jimmy returned MapKit error 4 and retains an approximate research anchor. Its hours and extended-reading permissions remain unknown. Observed English park naming and the Ayodya spelling are covered by identity regression tests.

The catalog applies these factual boundaries.

- Public park hours are separate from library-room hours.
- A quiet ambience description does not establish a silent-zone policy.
- Coffee service in a compound does not establish the same amenities for every tenant.
- A smoking-area restriction needs evidence for the named scope.
- Missing evidence remains unknown when applying hard requirements.

### 4.2 Recommendation comparison

Six profiles were compared across two time windows and three methods, producing **36 comparisons**.

The general comparator uses the empty-profile ordering after applying the target persona's factual and time gates. Category and combined methods use the application's recommendation entry point. Displayed proposals are checked after scheduling eligibility and venue deduplication.

The general baseline is reused across profile comparisons. Repeated elapsed-time values for that baseline do not represent additional requests. The empty-profile tie order is part of the current application behavior and initially favors the park entries in this catalog.

P4 and P5 were excluded from the development runner and first evaluated after the catalog and ranking policy hashes were frozen. This is a small, correlated technical sample. Reviewer judgments are stored separately from model inputs and recommendation execution.

### 4.3 Relevance acceptance protocol

Two independent reviewers must grade every persona/candidate pair using 0 for irrelevant/conflicting, 1 for partially relevant and 2 for strongly relevant. Each judgment requires a reason and candidate-specific sources. Reviewer materials omit method names and rankings. Disagreements require adjudication.

The agreed initial thresholds are as follows.

| Criterion | Threshold | Current assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical factual, identity and hard-gate violations | Zero in executed cases | Executed technical checks passed |
| Precision@3 | At least 2/3 for each adequately covered persona | Pending human grades |
| Mean NDCG@3 | At least 0.80 | Pending human grades |
| Mean category NDCG improvement over general | At least 0.10 | Pending human grades |
| Directed profile contrasts | At least 3 of 4 improve relevance for the new profile | Pending human grades |
| Relevant candidate coverage | At least three distinct eligible venues for a scored persona | Several profiles have sparse factual support |

Human Precision@3, NDCG@3 and satisfaction scores have not been generated. The scorer reports `insufficient_evidence` while required reviews, adjudication, coverage or time-window results are incomplete.

### Are the proposals generic?

The coffee/Wi-Fi ranking differs from the general control and is supported by documented coffee attributes. The reading category ranking retains two general park alternatives because supported reading coverage is narrow. E5 changes that list, but the added places lack documented reading support. Their presence therefore requires relevance review.

The outdoor profile overlaps the general control because the control already proposes parks. That overlap is consistent with the available attributes and requires interpretation together with relevance grades.

### Do the proposals meet personalized criteria?

The hard no-smoking restriction is enforced through the evidence gate. Its empty result reflects missing qualifying evidence. Soft-preference results show varying levels of factual support, with the strongest observed response in the coffee profile and limited coverage for reading and art/culture.

The current evidence supports **partial profile sensitivity**. Overall personalized relevance and any improvement from E5 remain unverified pending independent review.

### Local E5 measurement

Eight nonempty-profile/window combinations completed E5 ranking. General and no-match paths bypassed it. Repeated requests reuse cached vectors.

A separate fresh-pipeline coffee-profile measurement on Mac took **1.314 seconds** for the first recommendation and **0.00222 seconds** for the identical warm request. The warm result includes cache reuse. These values come from one Mac sequence.

## 8. Limitations and remaining acceptance

- Two independent reviewers must complete and adjudicate the blinded relevance judgments. The generated form contains 65 unrated persona/candidate pairs.
- The catalog includes shared complexes and five candidates with insufficient opening-hour evidence. Broader independent venue coverage is still needed.
- Reading, silent-zone, visual-art and no-smoking evidence is sparse.
- Physical network interruption/recovery and background/input-change interactions remain untested.
- E5 paraphrase quality requires the paired manual review described in the protocol. Category-level paraphrase invariants are covered by automated checks.
- Interactive rejection and export observation in the new mode require operator review alongside the scripted evidence.
- Holiday exceptions, actual venue access, queues, parking and observed journey completion remain unknown.
- User satisfaction, reduced planning effort, RAM/energy and population-level recommendation quality were not measured.

## 9. Conclusion

The executed POC demonstrates a working provider-backed pre-trip pipeline on the signed iPhone, including primary WeatherKit access, MapKit routing, reviewable proposals, persistence, duration revision and deadline conflict handling.

Recommendation results show that preference inputs can affect the proposed places. Coverage gaps also produce general alternatives or an explicit no-match response. The final personalization outcome remains **`insufficient_evidence`** until independent relevance judgments and adequate profile coverage are available.

The next evaluation work is to complete the blinded review, broaden the missing venue facts and execute the remaining physical-device interactions and field observations.
