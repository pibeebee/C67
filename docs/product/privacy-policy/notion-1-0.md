# Travelants Privacy & Security

Travelants is operated by Giovan Christoffel Sihombing. For questions about this policy or requests concerning your information, email [travelant.id@gmail.com](mailto:travelant.id@gmail.com).

## Your account

Travelants uses Sign in with Apple through Supabase Auth. Travelants uses your account ID, email address or Apple relay address, and authentication session to sign you in, keep your trips separate on your device, and authorize requests to Travelants services. You can choose Hide My Email when Apple offers it.

## Trip planning and location

Travelants uses the interests and places you select, your starting point, and your travel preferences to plan an itinerary. Saved itineraries are stored on the device where you save them.

Apple Maps processes place searches and the location inputs needed for travel estimates and route information. With your permission, Travelants uses your device location to find nearby places and detect arrival during an active trip. You can change this permission in Settings or enter a starting point manually. Travelants can use place coordinates to request weather information from Apple Weather.

Travelants can send selected Apple place identifiers to its Pocket service for opening-hour checks. The service authorizes these requests and forwards the requested place identifier to the opening-hours provider. Travelants 1.0 calculates schedules on the device with external AI assistance disabled.

## Support

If you email us, we use your address and the information you include to respond to the request. Avoid sending passwords, authentication codes, or unnecessary personal information.

## Storage and deletion

Travelants keeps your account record to provide account access. You can request deletion in **History → Account → Delete Account**. The app verifies your identity and guides you through removing Sign in with Apple access. When deletion completes, Travelants removes the account and associated records from its services and clears the account's saved itineraries on the device where you completed deletion.

You can remove individual trips through the app and revoke location access in Settings. For help with data deletion, email [travelant.id@gmail.com](mailto:travelant.id@gmail.com).
