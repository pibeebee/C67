Travelants Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026. Travelants is operated by Giovan Christoffel Sihombing.

Accounts

Travelants uses Sign in with Apple to authenticate you through Supabase Auth. Your account record and session are used to sign you in, keep your trips separate on the device, and authorize requests to the Travelants Pocket server. If you use Hide My Email, the service may receive an Apple relay address.

Trips, maps and location

Saved itineraries are stored in the app on your device. Travelants uses Apple Maps for place searches, travel estimates, and route information, which can include your search terms and relevant coordinates. You can select a starting point or let Travelants use your position. If you grant location access, Travelants can also use it to detect arrival during an active trip. You can change this permission in Settings.

Travelants sends selected Apple place identifiers through its Pocket service to the opening-hours service at maps-api.giovanc.com. Pocket checks your session and forwards the place identifier. It does not save the returned hours on the server. Travelants can also use place coordinates to obtain weather from Apple Weather. Version 1.0 calculates the itinerary on the device with external AI assistance disabled.

Apple provides Sign in with Apple, Maps, and Apple Weather. Supabase provides account authentication and storage. See Apple’s privacy policy and Supabase’s privacy policy.

Storage and deletion

Account records are kept while you have an account. Local itineraries stay on the device until you remove them or delete the app. If you email support, we keep your email and the information you send to handle the request. Operational logs and recovery backups are handled separately from live account records. Older copies in recovery backups can remain until those backups are removed. Contact us for information about retained records or to request deletion.

Use History → Account → Delete Account in the app to request deletion. Travelants removes the account and its associated records from Travelants services and clears its itineraries on the device that completes deletion. The account deletion flow guides you through removing Apple access, including instructions if you need to do so in your Apple Account settings.
