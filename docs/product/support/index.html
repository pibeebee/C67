Travelants Support

Get help with trip planning, saved itineraries, and your Travelants account.

Contact us

Email travelant.id@gmail.com for help or to share feedback. For a problem, include:

  • A short description and the steps you took.
  • Your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and Travelants app version if available.
  • A screenshot if helpful. Remove any private details before you send it.

Keep passwords and sign-in codes out of your message and screenshots.

Planning a trip

Pick the places you want to visit. You can choose a main spot or let Travelants decide. Travelants builds a schedule using travel times and opening hours and may suggest the next day when the trip cannot fit today. Review the plan before saving it.

Place search, travel estimates, and some place details need internet access. If planning fails, check your connection and try again. Verify estimates and time-sensitive details such as opening hours and travel conditions before leaving.

Location access

Travelants can use your location to find nearby places and detect arrival on an active trip. You can search for a starting point manually. To change location access, open Settings and find Travelants under Apps, or open Privacy & Security → Location Services → Travelants.

Saved trips

Your saved itineraries are stored on the device where you save them. Keep this in mind before uninstalling the app or switching devices.

Account and privacy

To delete your Travelants account, sign in and open History → Account → Delete Account. Follow the confirmation steps. See the Travelants Privacy Policy for information about data and privacy requests.
